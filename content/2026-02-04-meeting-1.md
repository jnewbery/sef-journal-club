## Papers

- [Economics of Long-Duration Energy Storage](https://example.org/paper1) — Patel et al., 2024

## Discussion Notes

Inaugural meeting. We reviewed the levelised cost methodology and debated the choice of discount rate. The group agreed that the 8% WACC assumption is optimistic for early-stage technologies.

Key points:

- Iron-air battery cost projections were seen as plausible but dependent on manufacturing scale
- The model does not account for revenue stacking (capacity market + balancing services)
- Comparison with pumped hydro LCOS needed sensitivity analysis on capacity factor
