The SEF Journal Club is a group of researchers and practitioners interested in discussing recent papers and developments in sustainable energy and energy systems.

We meet regularly to review and discuss selected papers, with a focus on fostering open technical debate.

### Format

Each meeting centres on one or more papers chosen by the group. The presenter gives a brief overview, followed by open discussion. Notes from each meeting are published here.

### Getting Involved

All are welcome. If you'd like to suggest a paper or present at a future meeting, please get in touch.
