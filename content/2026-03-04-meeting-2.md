## Papers

- [Demand Response Potential in Residential Buildings](https://example.org/paper2) — Jones & Li, 2024

## Discussion Notes

We discussed the modelling assumptions around thermostat setpoint flexibility and questioned whether the demand elasticity estimates hold at scale. The authors' grid frequency metric was considered a reasonable proxy but the group noted limitations in the dataset (UK only, 2019–2021).

Key points raised:

- Load diversity assumptions may overstate aggregate flexibility
- Interaction effects with EV charging not captured
- Rebound effects under-explored
